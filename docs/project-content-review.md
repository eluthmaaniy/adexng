## ZenroZone

- Inspected: https://zenrozone.com/ and https://zenrozone.com/products/dining-chairs
- Name evidence: homepage “About ZenroZone”; header wordmark is “Zenro”. Portfolio uses ZenroZone.
- Facts used: furniture, home essentials, gardening/tools, décor and lighting. Homepage shows room imagery, collection links, priced cards and quick-view controls. Dining Chairs detail has a multi-image gallery, dimensions/material specifications and shipping details.
- Audience: people furnishing or refreshing their homes, supported by homepage introduction.
- Screenshot source: `docs/screenshots/adex-zenrozone-home.jpg`; cover: `public/projects/zenrozone-homepage.webp`.
- Limitations: live store includes unfinished example-product text and inconsistent availability UI. Neither is promoted as a portfolio achievement. Homepage cookie overlay declined before final capture.
